01Research

I am a PhD student in Mathematical Finance at Texas Tech University, working with Svetlozar Rachev on machine learning for quantitative finance.

The through-line is simple to state and hard to do: take the parts of a market that look like noise — volatility, tail events, the microstructure of order flow — and ask which of them carry information that a model can actually learn, out of sample, without fooling itself. Alongside the academic work I co-founded DeepAI Finance, where the same models meet live portfolios.
