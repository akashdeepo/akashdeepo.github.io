1Abstract

I am a PhD student in Mathematical Finance at Texas Tech University, working under Dr. Svetlozar Rachev on machine learning applications in quantitative finance.

My research applies stochastic processes, statistical models, and machine learning to questions of asset pricing, market microstructure, and volatility — from Random Forest models trading at minute frequency to the rough, long-memory behavior of volatility itself. Alongside the academic work, I co-founded DeepAI Finance LLC, where the same models meet real markets.
